
# Security Risks of Crafting Allowlists for Phishing Awareness and Simulated Attacks

**Master's Thesis | Chalmers University of Technology | 2026**

## Overview

This research investigates security vulnerabilities introduced by allowlisting configurations used in phishing awareness training platforms. It explores how trusted email configurations can be exploited to bypass enterprise email filtering mechanisms.

The study focuses on header spoofing, shared IP infrastructure, and domain exposure through controlled cybersecurity experiments.

## Technologies & Tools

- Microsoft 365 / Exchange Online
- GoPhish – Phishing simulation framework
- Swaks – SMTP testing
- SendGrid – Shared email infrastructure
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC – Email authentication
- DNS / WHOIS – Domain analysis

## Research Methodology

- Designed and conducted controlled phishing simulations in Microsoft 365 environments.
- Tested header-based allowlisting vulnerabilities and email filtering bypass techniques.
- Investigated security risks associated with shared IP infrastructure.
- Analyzed domain registration and potential exposure across phishing awareness platforms.
- Evaluated experimental results and identified possible security improvements.

## Key Findings

- Demonstrated how spoofable email headers can bypass spam filtering under vulnerable configurations.
- Identified risks associated with IP-based allowlisting on shared email infrastructure.
- Found no unregistered primary vendor domains among those examined.
- Observed that malware protection remained effective in the tested scenarios.
- Proposed mitigation strategies to strengthen enterprise email security.
